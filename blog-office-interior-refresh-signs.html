5 Signs Your Office Interior Needs a Refresh

August 19, 2026 5 min read InterioArty Design Team

Most office refresh conversations start with something obvious - a leaking ceiling tile, worn carpet, a broken chair nobody's replaced. But by the time the obvious signs show up, the space has usually been quietly working against your team for a while. Here are five signs we look for when a client asks us to walk through their office.

01 Meeting Rooms Are Always Booked (or Never Used)

If your team is constantly fighting over one meeting room, or - just as tellingly - has a meeting room nobody ever books, that's a sign the room count and format no longer match how your team actually works. A single large conference room might have made sense for a 10-person team; it doesn't for a 40-person team that mostly needs quick huddles.

The fix isn't always more rooms - sometimes it's converting one large room into two smaller, more flexible spaces.

02 People Choose to Sit Anywhere But Their Desk

Walk through your office at 3pm. If people are working from the pantry, a stairwell, or a corner near the window instead of their assigned desk, that's not a discipline problem - it's a design problem. People vote with their bodies for spaces with better light, less noise, or more comfort.

This is one of the clearest, most honest signals we look for during a walkthrough, because it tells you exactly what part of the office is genuinely working.

03 Clients Comment on the Space, Not the Work

If a client visit ends with a comment about the office itself - good or bad - rather than the meeting's outcome, the space is drawing attention away from what you actually want them focused on. A well-designed office should feel confident and quietly professional, not distracting in either direction.

04 Your Brand Isn't Anywhere in the Room

A generic office - beige walls, standard-issue furniture, no visible connection to what your company actually does or stands for - sends a subtle signal to both employees and visiting clients that the space was an afterthought. This is one of the fastest, most cost-effective fixes: bringing brand colours and materials into the physical space doesn't require a full renovation.

05 Basic Maintenance Has Become a Weekly Task

If your facilities team is regularly patching the same issues - a sticking door, flickering lights in one section, a desk that wobbles no matter how many times it's tightened - it's often cheaper in the long run to address the underlying fit-out than to keep maintaining an ageing one.

“The clearest sign your office needs a refresh isn't a complaint - it's watching where people choose to sit when they have a choice.”

The Takeaway

None of these five signs on their own means you need a full office renovation - but if two or three sound familiar, it's worth a proper walkthrough. Often the fix is more targeted (and more affordable) than people assume: better zoning, updated lighting, and a stronger brand presence can solve most of what's listed here without touching the whole floor.
