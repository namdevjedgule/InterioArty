Our Story

From one home, to hundreds.

It all started in 2012 when Saurabh and Manu Aneja began to fix up their ancestral home. They wanted to make it look modern and friendly, so they considered everything - from the rooms to the furniture, the kitchen, and even the colours of the walls. Unfortunately, the interior designers they hired before performed poorly.

With time, they discovered that the interior design world needed clarity and better organisation.

That realisation led them to start InterioArty - a studio that offers personalised, modern interior design options and ensures the work gets done smoothly. As a team of interior decor professionals, we work on ideating and transforming home and commercial spaces without much hassle.
