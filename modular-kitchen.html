Layouts We Design

Types of kitchen layout.

L-Shaped Kitchen

Maximise corner spaces with an L-shaped kitchen, offering a seamless workflow and spacious feel for modern interiors.

U-Shaped Kitchen Layout

A U-shaped kitchen provides ample storage and countertop space, perfect for large kitchens and efficient cooking.

Island Kitchen Design

The island kitchen adds extra workspace, storage, and seating, creating a focal point ideal for open-plan kitchens.

Peninsula Kitchen Layout

A peninsula kitchen offers additional counter space, combining functionality with a compact design for smaller spaces.

Galley Kitchen Design

The galley kitchen is a streamlined, efficient layout with two parallel countertops, perfect for small or narrow kitchens.

One-Wall Kitchen Layout

A one-wall kitchen is a minimalist design with everything arranged along a single wall, ideal for compact spaces and simplicity.

G-Shaped Kitchen Design

A G-shaped kitchen offers extended counter space and storage, ideal for larger, open kitchens.

Parallel Kitchen Layout

A parallel kitchen provides two facing counters, perfect for efficient cooking in compact spaces.
