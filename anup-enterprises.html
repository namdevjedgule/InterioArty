Anup Enterprises HQ

A growing team had outgrown a generic fit-out. We planned the space around how the team works and built the brand into the materials.

The brief.

A fast-growing team had outgrown their old, generic office fit-out - open desks with no real zoning, one meeting room for the whole floor, and no sense of brand identity anywhere.

We planned workstations, private cabins and a proper conference room around how the team actually works day to day, and built the company's brand colours and materials into the space itself, rather than treating branding as wall decals added at the end.

What we delivered.

  • Workstations & private cabins
  • Dedicated meeting & conference rooms
  • Branding-led material & colour palette
  • Acoustic zoning for focus areas
  • Cable management & IT-ready desking
  • Fast turnaround for business continuity
