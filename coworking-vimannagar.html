A Co-Working Studio Fit-Out

A shared workspace built to handle many different tenants and a lot of daily foot traffic without feeling generic.

The brief.

Co-working spaces have to work harder than a single-tenant office - different companies, different work styles, and far more daily foot traffic.

We designed flexible hot-desk zones alongside a handful of bookable private cabins, used durable, easy-to-clean materials throughout, and leaned into an industrial-warm palette so the space felt distinctive rather than like a generic serviced office.

What we delivered.

  • Flexible hot-desk & private cabin zones
  • Bookable meeting rooms with AV wiring
  • Durable, high-traffic flooring & finishes
  • Distinctive industrial-warm material palette
  • Shared pantry & breakout lounge
  • Scalable layout for future expansion
