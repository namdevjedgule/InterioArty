1. Introduction

InterioArty ("we", "us", "our") designs and delivers modular kitchens, full home interiors, office interiors and renovation projects across Pune. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or enquire about our services, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using our website or submitting an enquiry form, you agree to the collection and use of information in line with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly - your name, phone number, email address, project type, and any details you share through our enquiry forms, chat widget, or phone calls.
  • Information collected automatically - your IP address, browser type, device information, pages visited, and how you interact with our site, collected via cookies and similar technologies.
  • Information from third parties - if you reach us through a partner platform, referral, or advertising channel, we may receive limited information about that referral source.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to your enquiries and schedule consultations or site visits.
  • Prepare quotes, design proposals, and project timelines.
  • Improve our website, services, and customer experience.
  • Send you project updates, offers, or newsletters (only if you've opted in).
  • Comply with legal obligations and protect against fraud or misuse.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to remember your preferences, understand site traffic, and improve performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, though some parts of the site (like the enquiry form) may not work as intended without them.

5. How We Share Information

We may share your information with:

  • Our internal design and execution teams, so they can plan and deliver your project.
  • Trusted vendors and contractors involved in fabrication, installation, or logistics for your project.
  • Service providers who help us operate our website, send communications, or process payments, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities, if required by law.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this policy - including ongoing project support and warranty obligations - unless a longer retention period is required by law.

8. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of your information, subject to legal or contractual limits.
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 11.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for adults seeking interior design and renovation services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when it was last revised.
