Why VR Walkthroughs Are Changing How Pune Homeowners Design

July 20, 2026 4 min read InterioArty Design Team

A few years ago, VR walkthroughs were a premium add-on reserved for large commercial projects. Now, we include one free with most 2BHK and 3BHK full-home projects - not as a gimmick, but because it genuinely changes how confidently clients approve a design before a single wall is built.

01 What a VR Walkthrough Actually Is

Once your layout and material choices are finalised, our design team builds a fully navigable 3D model of your home that you can walk through using a VR headset - turning your head to look around, walking from room to room, and seeing your actual chosen colours, finishes, and furniture placement in scale, before any construction begins.

02 Why 2D Drawings Don't Tell the Full Story

A floor plan tells you where a wall is. It doesn't tell you how it feels to stand in your kitchen and look toward the living room, or whether a corridor feels cramped when you're actually walking through it rather than looking at it from above. Most people simply can't fully translate a 2D drawing into a lived spatial experience - and that's not a failure of imagination, it's just how spatial perception works.

03 Catching Problems Before They're Built

The single biggest value of a VR walkthrough is catching issues while they're still free to fix. Clients regularly notice things in VR they didn't catch on a drawing - a wardrobe door that would swing awkwardly into a walkway, a kitchen island that feels tighter than expected, a colour that looks completely different at real scale than it did on a small sample chip.

04 Changing Materials Without Changing the Budget Mid-Project

Because the walkthrough happens before procurement and fabrication begin, changes made after seeing the VR model cost nothing beyond a design revision. Compare that to realising you don't like a countertop colour after it's already been cut and installed - at that point, a change means real added cost and delay.

05 Who Benefits Most From This

It's particularly valuable for clients who are naturally less confident reading floor plans, for couples or families making joint decisions (since everyone can experience the same walkthrough rather than relying on one person's interpretation of a drawing), and for larger, more complex full-home projects where there's simply more surface area for a misunderstanding to hide in.

06 What to Expect From Your Walkthrough

After your design and material selections are confirmed, our team typically delivers a VR walkthrough within a short turnaround, and schedules a session for you to experience it in person before giving final sign-off. It's genuinely one of the more enjoyable parts of the design process for most clients.

“A floor plan tells you where the wall is. A VR walkthrough tells you how it feels to actually stand in the room.”

The Takeaway

VR walkthroughs aren't about the novelty of the technology - they're about giving you a genuine, confident preview of your home before it's built, so the changes you'd otherwise make after installation get made for free, on a screen, weeks earlier.
