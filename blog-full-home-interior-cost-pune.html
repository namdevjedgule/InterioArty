How Much Does a Full Home Interior Really Cost in Pune?

August 26, 2026 9 min read InterioArty Design Team

This is the question we get asked before almost any other: "what will it actually cost?" Most quotes you'll find online are either vague ranges designed to get you into a showroom, or so specific they assume a level of finish you never asked for. Here's a straighter answer, based on what we've actually delivered across dozens of Pune homes this year.

Every number below assumes a mid-range material palette - not the cheapest board available, not premium imported finishes. Your actual number will move depending on layout complexity, material choices, and how much of the home you're touching.

01 What Actually Goes Into the Cost

A full home interior quote isn't one number - it's several line items stacked together: modular furniture (wardrobes, kitchen, TV units), false ceiling and electrical work, painting, flooring (if included), and soft furnishings. Most Pune interior companies price primarily on modular carpentry, since that's usually the single largest line item.

When you compare quotes, check whether false ceiling, painting, and electrical work are included or quoted separately - this is the most common reason two quotes for the "same" home look wildly different.

02 1 BHK Ballpark Numbers

For a typical 1BHK (450-650 sq.ft) with a modular kitchen, wardrobes in one or two bedrooms, and a TV unit, expect a mid-range budget in the ₹4L-₹7L range. This usually covers essential modular furniture and basic false ceiling in the living area, without a full home renovation of walls, flooring, or extensive lighting design.

03 2 BHK Ballpark Numbers

A 2BHK (750-1,100 sq.ft) with a full modular kitchen, wardrobes across both bedrooms, a TV unit, and moderate false ceiling and lighting typically lands between ₹8L and ₹14L. This is the most common project size we handle, and it's where design choices - like adding a breakfast counter or upgrading finishes - start to meaningfully move the number.

04 3 BHK Ballpark Numbers

For a 3BHK (1,200-1,700 sq.ft) - similar in scope to our Wellington Vista project - a full turnkey interior with consistent finishes across every room usually falls between ₹15L and ₹24L, depending on how much false ceiling, custom furniture, and premium finishes are involved.

05 What Pushes the Budget Up

The biggest budget movers we see aren't the obvious ones. Custom-shaped furniture (curved units, non-standard wardrobe heights), premium hardware (soft-close everything, imported profiles), extensive false ceiling with cove lighting, and high-end countertop material choices each add up faster than most people expect.

Structural changes - moving a wall, shifting a kitchen's plumbing point, or altering the electrical layout significantly - also add cost that has nothing to do with the furniture itself.

06 Hidden Costs People Forget

A few costs regularly catch first-time renovators off guard: society NOC and deposit fees for carrying out work in an apartment complex, temporary storage if you need to move furniture out during execution, and GST, which applies on top of the quoted project value in most cases.

Ask your designer to walk you through exactly what's included versus what's separate before you sign - a good quote should make this distinction clear upfront, not after work has started.

07 Where You Can Save Without Cutting Corners

The easiest place to save without compromising quality is standardising cabinet sizes wherever possible instead of custom-fabricating every unit, and being selective about where you spend on premium finishes - typically the kitchen and living room get the most daily visibility, so it's reasonable to prioritise budget there over a guest bedroom wardrobe, for instance.

08 How Payment Milestones Usually Work

Most Pune interior companies, including us, follow a milestone-based payment structure: a booking advance, a payment at design confirmation, another at material procurement, and a final payment at handover. This protects both sides - you're not paying the full amount upfront, and the company isn't fully financing your project's materials themselves.

“The biggest budget mistake isn't overspending on materials - it's not knowing what's included in a quote until the invoice arrives.”

The Takeaway

There's no single "right" number for a full home interior - it depends entirely on your home's size, layout complexity, and material choices. The most useful thing you can do before getting quotes is walk through this article's breakdown room by room, so you can tell a realistic quote from an inflated or artificially low one.

If you'd like a real, itemised estimate for your specific home rather than a ballpark, that's a conversation worth having directly.
