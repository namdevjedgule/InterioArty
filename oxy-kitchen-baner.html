The Oxy Residence Kitchen

An efficient, inviting kitchen that combines functionality with style - built around how this family actually cooks, not a showroom layout.

The brief.

The clients came to us with a small, closed-off kitchen that felt cramped despite having decent storage. Our brief was to make it feel open, keep the working triangle tight, and give them a place to actually enjoy cooking together.

We reworked the layout into an efficient L-shape, added a breakfast counter for casual seating, and used a two-tone laminate finish to keep the space feeling bright without showing every fingerprint.

What we delivered.

  • Soft-close cabinets & drawers
  • Storage tailored to daily cooking habits
  • Two-tone laminate & acrylic finish
  • Chimney, hob & sink layout replanned
  • Breakfast counter with bar seating
  • Under-cabinet LED task lighting
