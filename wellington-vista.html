Wellington Vista - A 3BHK Transformation

A young family wanted a home that felt calm, contemporary and easy to maintain - so we carried one material palette through every room.

The brief.

The brief was simple on paper and demanding in practice: a home that felt calm, contemporary, and easy to live in day to day. We reworked the layout for better natural light and flow between the living and dining areas.

We carried one considered material palette - warm woods, matte finishes, soft neutrals - through every room, so the whole home reads as a single space rather than a collection of separately decorated rooms.

What we delivered.

  • Living room & bedroom interiors
  • False ceiling & layered lighting design
  • Wardrobes & custom storage throughout
  • Consistent material palette, room to room
  • Turnkey execution, start to finish
  • Post-handover snag-list support
