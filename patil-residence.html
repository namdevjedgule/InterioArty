The Patil Residence Refresh

A 12-year-old home with good bones and tired finishes - refreshed without a full structural rebuild.

The brief.

This home had good bones - solid layout, decent natural light - but 12 years of wear had left the finishes tired and the electrical and plumbing lines due for an upgrade.

Rather than a full teardown, we planned a phased renovation: structural tweaks where they mattered, an electrical and plumbing upgrade behind the walls, and entirely refreshed finishes throughout, so the family could keep living in parts of the home while other sections were being worked on.

What we delivered.

  • Structural & civil changes where needed
  • Electrical & plumbing line upgrades
  • Refreshed finishes, no full rebuild
  • Phased execution around family's schedule
  • Budget-friendly material selection
  • Minimal disruption to daily routine
